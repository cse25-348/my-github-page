Mental Wellness

Managing Stress

Stress is a normal part of university life, but it does not have to take over. Understanding what causes your stress and learning how to manage it can make a huge difference to your wellbeing, your focus, and your overall university experience.

What is Stress?

Stress is your body's natural response to pressure or demands. In small amounts it can motivate you, but too much stress over a long period can affect your health, sleep, and ability to concentrate. Recognising stress early is the first step to managing it well.
