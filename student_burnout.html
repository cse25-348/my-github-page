Mental Wellness

Student Burnout

Student burnout is more common than you think. The constant pressure of assignments, exams, part-time jobs, and personal responsibilities can slowly drain your energy and motivation. Recognising burnout early and knowing how to respond can protect both your health and your academic performance.

What is Burnout?

Burnout is a state of physical, emotional, and mental exhaustion caused by prolonged stress. Unlike regular tiredness, burnout does not go away with a single good night's sleep. It builds up over time when you push yourself too hard without enough rest or support. Students experiencing burnout often feel detached, hopeless, and unable to engage with their studies even when they want to.

Signs of Burnout

Constant Exhaustion

Loss of Motivation

Difficulty Concentrating

Feeling Detached

Causes

  • Overloaded schedule
  • Lack of sleep
  • No personal time
  • Unrealistic expectations

How to Recover

Rest Without Guilt

Set Boundaries

Talk
